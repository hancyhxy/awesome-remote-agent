# A video edited only through chat

A ten-minute talk cut with picture-in-picture screens and motion graphics, every step done by chatting with an agent in the cloud.

- Original: [@mattyp on X](https://x.com/mattyp/status/2109475664629772522) (♥ 446 likes, 27.8k views, made with Grok, cloud workspaces)
- Prompt by Hey Atu, based on the post.

## What you get

- A long talk cut into sections with picture-in-picture screens
- Motion graphics for the diagrams you describe
- No editing app: every change is a chat message

## Prompt

```text
Here is a [length] recording of me talking: [file], and the screen recordings I mention: [folder].

Cut it into sections. When I talk about a screen, show it full-frame with me as a small picture-in-picture in the corner. Turn the diagrams I describe into simple motion graphics. Give me a contact sheet of every shot with its timecode so I can ask for changes by number.
```

Paste it into a chat with [Hey Atu](https://heyatu.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=awesome-remote-agent&utm_content=chat-only-edit), or into Claude Code, Codex or Pi.
