# An ad cut from 2,000 conference clips

The agent watched every clip from a conference, tagged the usable shots and cut them into ads. An honest post about what it got wrong.

- Original: [@Mike_Stelzner on X](https://x.com/Mike_Stelzner/status/2112121588317344197) (♥ 8 likes, 765 views, made with Claude, Gemini)
- Prompt by Hey Atu, based on the post.

## What you get

- A searchable library of every clip with key moments
- Ads with b-roll picked from your own footage
- A shortlist you approve before anything is cut

## Prompt

```text
My event footage is in [folder]: about [number] clips.

1. Watch every clip and log it: who is in it, what happens, the best 3-second moments, and whether it is usable (focus, framing, sound).
2. Make a searchable page of the clips with thumbnails and key moments.
3. Propose b-roll for my ad script [script]: three options per line, from usable clips only.
4. Wait for me to approve the shortlist, then cut a 30-second 9:16 ad with captions.
```
