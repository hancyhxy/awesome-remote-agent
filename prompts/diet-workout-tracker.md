# A diet and workout tracker

Meals, macros and workouts in one app, with soft colours and friendly motion.

- Original: [Musemind on Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/shots/20243423-Diet-Workout-Tracker-App-Design-Nutrition-Mobile-App-UI-UX) (♥ 415 likes, 154k views)
- Written by Hey Atu for this pick.

## What you get

- A daily ring for calories and bars for protein, carbs and fat
- Log a meal by sending a photo or a sentence to Atu
- Workouts from your watch next to what you ate
- A Hey Atu Space on your phone, or a standalone web app
