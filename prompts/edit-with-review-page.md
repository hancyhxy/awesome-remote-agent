# An AI edit with a review page for notes

The agent edited a whole video about itself, then put it on a review page where you leave notes at a frame and it re-cuts.

- Original: [@mrpaidsocial on X](https://x.com/mrpaidsocial/status/2116281757955723456) (♥ 0 likes, 91 views, made with Claude Code, ElevenLabs)
- Prompt by Hey Atu, based on the post.

## What you get

- A full edit with captions, music and motion graphics
- A review page: play the cut, leave a note at any frame
- A new version after each round of notes

## Prompt

```text
Edit my footage in [folder] into a 9:16 video about [topic], in the style of [reference video].

Then make a review page: the cut on the left, my notes on the right. I can pause at any frame and leave a note. When I say "next round", apply every open note, render V2, and show what changed for each note.
```

Paste it into a chat with [Hey Atu](https://heyatu.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=awesome-remote-agent&utm_content=edit-with-review-page), or into Claude Code, Codex or Pi.
