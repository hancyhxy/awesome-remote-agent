# A fitness app with playful motion

Bold cards, big numbers and springy transitions between a workout list and a live session.

- Original: [tubik on Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/shots/9809369-Fitness-App-UI-Animation) (♥ 767 likes, 268k views)
- Written by Hey Atu for this pick.

## What you get

- A workout list and a live-session screen with the same motion language
- Your real workouts from your watch or a log you keep with Atu
- A Hey Atu Space on your phone, or a standalone web app

## Prompt

```text
Build me a workout app with playful motion, in the style of tubik's Fitness App UI Animation (https://dribbble.com/shots/9809369-Fitness-App-UI-Animation).

Data: my workouts from my watch (ask which one and connect it on my Mac), plus anything I tell you in chat ("did 3x10 squats at 60 kg"). Use sample data until it is connected.

Screens:
1. This week: a stack of bold colour cards, one per workout type, each with minutes and a progress bar to my weekly goal.
2. Session: tap a card and it expands into a full-screen session with a big timer, heart rate and the next exercise.
3. History: a calendar with a dot per workout day.

Motion: cards expand from where they were tapped, numbers count up, progress bars fill with a little overshoot. Keep every animation under 400 ms.

Make it a Hey Atu Space I can open on my phone.
```
