# A health tracker dashboard

One screen with heart rate, sleep, steps and calories, each as a clean card that animates in.

- Original: [Sigma Software Design on Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/shots/24693286-Health-Tracker-Dashboard-UI-UX-Design) (♥ 421 likes, 237k views)
- Written by Hey Atu for this pick.

## What you get

- A one-page dashboard: heart rate, sleep, steps, calories and weekly trends
- Your own wearable data, refreshed every morning
- A morning summary in Updates, with the dashboard one tap away

## Prompt

```text
Build me a health dashboard in the style of Sigma Software Design's Health Tracker (https://dribbble.com/shots/24693286-Health-Tracker-Dashboard-UI-UX-Design).

Data: my wearable data (ask which device and connect it on my Mac), synced every morning. Use sample data until it is connected.

Layout: a greeting and today's date, then a grid of cards: heart rate (live-style line), sleep (hours and stages bar), steps (ring to my goal), calories (bar per day this week), and a weekly trend card that compares this week with last week.

Look: light, airy, generous white space, one accent colour per metric, rounded cards, large numbers. Cards fade and rise in one after another; charts draw from left to right.

Make it a Hey Atu Space I can open on my phone, and send me a short morning summary in Updates that links to it. Not medical advice.
```
