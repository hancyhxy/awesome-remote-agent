# Your wearable's app, without the subscription

A warm, calm health app for a WHOOP band: recovery ring, heart rate, sleep, strain and stress on one Today screen.

- Original: [OpenStrap on GitHub](https://github.com/OpenStrap/edge) (★ 924 stars)
- Written by Hey Atu for this pick.

## What you get

- A Today screen with a recovery ring and four metric cards with sparklines
- Sleep, heart rate and workout screens
- Your own data from Fitbit, WHOOP, Oura, Garmin or Apple Health, synced to your Mac every morning
- A Hey Atu Space on your phone, or a standalone web app

## Prompt

```text
Build me a personal health app in the style of OpenStrap Edge (https://github.com/OpenStrap/edge).

Data: use my own wearable data. Ask me which device I wear (Fitbit, WHOOP, Oura, Garmin or Apple Watch), set up the connection on my Mac, and sync it every morning. Until it is connected, use 30 days of realistic sample data.

2. Sleep: last night's stages as a timeline, the 14-night trend.
3. Heart rate: resting heart rate and HRV over 30 days.
4. Workouts: a list with duration, average heart rate and load.

Look: warm off-white background, white rounded cards with soft shadows, one coral accent, big tabular numbers, small labels. Numbers count up and rings draw in when a screen opens. Offer a dark version too.
```
