The agent cuts the video, then hands back a Premiere timeline: picture on one track, sound effects grouped, music left for you.

## What you get

- A finished edit plus an XML timeline that opens in Premiere
- One clip per track, mono audio files, every file starting at 0:00
- The parts you still want to do by hand left open
