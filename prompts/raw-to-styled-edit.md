# Raw talking head in, styled edit out

One raw clip goes in; the agent adds type, camera moves and transitions, and a video model rebuilds the scene with lips still in sync.

- Original: [@emmanuel_2m on X](https://x.com/emmanuel_2m/status/2107778292824564122) (♥ 59 likes, 4.9k views, made with Claude Opus 5.5, Scenario MCP, Seedance 2.5)
- Prompt by Hey Atu, based on the post.

## What you get

- A vertical edit of one talking-head clip with type, camera moves and transitions
- Optional restyled backgrounds from a video model
- The raw and the edit side by side to compare

## Prompt

```text
Here is one raw talking-head clip: [file]. Edit it into a punchy 9:16 video.

1. Transcribe it and cut the pauses, false starts and filler.
2. Add kinetic captions: one to three words at a time, the key word larger.
3. Do the After Effects part in code: punch-in camera moves on key lines, title cards, transitions between sections.
4. If a video model is available, restyle the background for two or three moments, keeping my lips in sync.
5. Render a side-by-side of raw and edit so I can compare, then the final MP4.
```
