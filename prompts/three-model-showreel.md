# Three openers, three models, one prompt each

A hand-drawn story, a kinetic-type showreel and a recipe with live counters, side by side.

- Original: [@N01ennn on X](https://x.com/N01ennn/status/2108520767092597182) (♥ 1.5k likes, 121.7k views, made with Claude Opus, Sonnet and Haiku 5.5)
- Prompt by the post by @N01ennn.

## What you get

- An opener in one of three styles: hand-drawn story, kinetic type or step-by-step
- 15 to 50 seconds, rendered to MP4
- Your title, logo and colours

## Prompt

```text
Pick one:

1. A hand-drawn story: a little agent wakes up at its desk, gets feedback notes, fixes them, ends on heart eyes and a green check. 53 s.
2. A kinetic type showreel: six easing curves, a morphing star, particles, blurred type, "every frame written in code". 15 s.
3. A step-by-step White Russian recipe: ice, vodka, coffee liqueur, cream, with live counters and a checklist. 30 s.

Swap the subject for your own: your channel, your product or your recipe.
```
